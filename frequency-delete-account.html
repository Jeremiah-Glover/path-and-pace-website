Delete Your Frequency Account

Last updated: October 9, 2026

You can delete your Frequency account, and the information that goes with it, at any time.

Frequency is owned by It's Frequency LLC, which is responsible for your account and data. The app is developed and distributed by Path & Pace LLC.

Delete your account in the app

This is the fastest way, and it takes effect right away.

  1. Open Frequency on your iPhone or Android phone, or sign in to the web portal.
  2. Go to your profile, the Mirror.
  3. Tap Delete account, then Delete my account to confirm.

Your account and its data are deleted immediately, and you are signed out on that device. This can't be undone.

Request deletion without the app

You don't need the app to delete your account. Email us and we'll take care of it.

Email a deletion request

Send your request to support@path-and-pace.com from the email address on your account (or tell us the name you joined with and whether you signed in with Apple or Google). We verify the request is yours, delete the account within 30 days, and reply to confirm.

What gets deleted, what stays, and how long it takes

Deleted from our servers

  • Your sign-in.
  • Your name, date of birth, city and state.
  • Your page, check-ins, practice days, points and notification settings.
  • Your RSVPs (your seats go back), attendance, pod place, board posts and answers to prompts.
  • Your devices' push-notification tokens.

What stays

Nothing that identifies you. Anonymous poll counts stay as they are, because a vote was never stored against a name.

Timing and retention

When your account is deleted, your data is removed from our systems right away. We don't keep separate copies of our own — any residual data lives only in the routine backups kept by our infrastructure provider, Google Firebase, and is purged on Google's standard backup schedule.

Hosts

If you are the society's only host, make another member a host first, so the society is not left with nobody to run it. The app will remind you.
