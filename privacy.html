Privacy Policy

Effective August 31, 2026  ·  Path & Pace LLC, Atlanta, Georgia

This policy covers path-and-pace.com and the internal messaging systems Path & Pace operates. Individual products have their own policies, linked at the bottom of this page.

What we collect

When you use the contact form

If you send us a message through the form on our site, we receive the name, email address, and message text you type into it. Nothing else. We use it to reply to you.

When you visit the site

Our hosting provider records standard server request data — IP address, browser type, pages requested, timestamps — as a normal part of serving a website and protecting it from abuse. We do not run advertising trackers, analytics pixels, or third-party cookies on this site.

Internal notifications

Path & Pace operates an internal assistant that sends operational summaries and alerts by email and SMS. Recipients are the business owner and authorized collaborators working on the platform. Each recipient is enrolled by signing a written consent form at onboarding, which records the mobile number and the consent language; Path & Pace retains the signed copy. The blank form is published at Text Message Consent.

There is no public signup for these messages and no keyword to text. Visitors and customers are not enrolled and cannot be. Message frequency varies with account activity. Reply STOP to any message to stop receiving them, or HELP for assistance.

What we don't do

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not share it with advertisers or data brokers.
  • We do not add you to a mailing list because you contacted us.
  • We do not send marketing text messages.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Information sharing to subcontractors in support services, such as customer service, is permitted. All other use case categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

Who else touches your data

We use a small number of service providers to run the site. Each receives only what it needs to do its job:

  • Cloudflare — hosting and delivery of the website.
  • Web3Forms — delivers contact form submissions to our inbox.
  • Twilio — carries the internal SMS notifications described above.

We do not authorize any of these providers to use your information for their own purposes.

How long we keep things

Contact form messages stay in our email until they are no longer useful for supporting you, and are deleted after that. Server logs are retained on our provider's standard schedule and are not used to build profiles. Signed consent forms and records of opt-out requests are retained for as long as required to demonstrate compliance.

Your choices

You can ask us what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Write to support@path-and-pace.com and we will respond. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under laws such as the GDPR or the California Consumer Privacy Act; we honor those requests regardless of where you are.

To stop receiving text messages from our number, reply STOP to any message. Reply HELP for assistance. Message and data rates may apply.

Children

This site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has sent us information, contact us and we will remove it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date at the top of the page. Material changes will be noted here rather than made quietly.
