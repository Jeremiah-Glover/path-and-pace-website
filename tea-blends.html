ON RATIONING About 160 grams across twelve jars — call it sixty cups if nobody gets greedy. Rooibos, orange peel, lemon peel and elderberry are the ones with real volume. Lemon balm, both roses and the licorice are two or three cups each, so those are pinches, not scoops.

TAP ANYTHING TO SEE WHAT IT DOES

WHAT'S MISSING Ginger and cinnamon — between them they cover warmth, which nothing on this table has. Cacao nibs would give the Gamma cup a reason to exist. Hibiscus for tartness and colour. Lavender and tulsi round out the sleepy end.
