Privacy Policy.

How Path & Pace LLC collects, uses, and protects your information in Boss Up — The App. Written for humans, not billed by the hour.

Last updated: July 7, 2026

A quick heads-up. This policy is written to be clear and complete, but it isn't legal advice, and we're not lawyers. We recommend having it reviewed by qualified counsel before you publish it.

The short version

  • You need an account to use the app — just an email and a password (the password is handled by our sign-in provider; we never see it).

  • You can delete your account in the app — Settings → Delete account — or by emailing us.
  • Who we share with: Google Firebase (our backend) and Apple (the app platform). That's it.

01

Who we are

This Privacy Policy explains how Path & Pace LLC ("we," "us," or "our") handles your information when you use Boss Up — The App (the "App"), our native mobile app for iOS (with Android planned). Path & Pace LLC is the publisher and distributor of the App.

The App delivers the book Boss Up plus an interactive, gamified "employee onboarding" experience set in a fictional store called TonyMart — including reading, a community chat, stats, and saved highlights. An account is required to use the App.

For the purposes of data-protection law (including the GDPR), Path & Pace LLC is the "data controller" for the App. If you have any questions about this policy or your data, contact us at jeremiahglover@path-and-pace.com.

One clarification about brands. The Boss Up book and brand website (bossupthebook.com) are operated by a separate company, Tony Talks Media LLC. This policy covers only the App, which is published and distributed by Path & Pace LLC. It does not cover the book website or any purchases made there.

02

Summary — the short version

Here's the whole thing in plain English. The sections below add the detail.

  • You create an account with an email and password. Our sign-in provider (Google Firebase Authentication) handles the password — we never store or see it in readable form.
  • By default, the App keeps your data on your device. You can read the book, track progress, save highlights, and set preferences without any of it leaving your phone.
  • If you want cloud features — syncing your profile across devices and joining the community "Break Room" chat — you opt in with a data-sharing setting that's off by default. Turn it back off and we delete the synced copy from our servers.
  • We use Google Firebase for our backend and Apple as the app platform. We do not use advertising networks, third-party analytics/tracking SDKs, and we do not sell or rent your data.
  • The App has no in-app purchases and processes no payment data.
  • You can delete your account right inside the App, and you have privacy rights you can exercise any time by emailing us.
03

Information we collect

Information you provide

  • Email address — used for your account login.
  • Password — handled by our authentication provider, Google Firebase Authentication. The App never stores or sees your password in readable form.
  • Display name — the name shown on your profile and in the community chat.
  • Phone number — optional.
  • Profile picture — optional. You can pick a built-in icon, or choose a photo from your device's photo library using your operating system's photo picker. We only ask for photo-library access at the moment you choose to add a photo.
  • Age attestation — you confirm you are at least 13 years old. We store the fact that you confirmed and when. The App is not directed to children under 13.

Usage & content

  • Reading progress — which chapters and assignments you've completed.
  • Reading preferences — theme, text size, and page-turn settings.
  • Saved highlights & quotes — passages you mark while reading. These are stored on your device.
  • Community messages — content you post in the in-app community chat (the "Break Room"), which is visible to other eligible users. If you report or block another user, we use those reports and blocks for safety and moderation.

Created automatically

  • Anonymous sign-in identity — on first launch, the App creates an anonymous authentication identity so it works before you sign up. When you register, this is upgraded to your email account.
  • Push token — if you enable notifications, a device push token is generated to deliver messages (see Push notifications).

We do not collect your camera, precise location, contacts, or microphone data. We do not use advertising IDs or third-party tracking SDKs.

How we use your information

We use your information only to run the App and its features:

  • To create and secure your account — authenticating your login and keeping your account safe.
  • To deliver the book and your experience — showing content, tracking your reading progress, remembering your preferences, and saving your highlights.
  • To sync your data across devices — but only if you've turned on the data-sharing setting (see below).
  • To run the community chat — showing your messages and display name to other eligible users, and handling reports and blocks for safety.
  • To send notifications — only if you've enabled them.
  • To keep the App safe and working — moderating content, preventing abuse, and fixing problems.
  • To meet legal obligations — where the law requires it.
05

Your consent & the data-sharing setting

This is the heart of how the App treats your privacy, so we want to be clear about it.

  • By default, the App does not sync your personal data to the cloud. Reading the book and using your library work fully on your device without sharing anything.
  • You must explicitly opt in. There's a "data sharing" setting that is OFF by default. Only when you switch it on do we sync your profile (like your name and reading progress) to our backend.
  • Opting in is also what unlocks the community "Break Room" chat, which is a cloud feature.
  • You can withdraw consent any time. Turning the setting back OFF purges the synced copy of your data from our servers.

Legal basis (GDPR). For users in the EU and UK, the lawful basis for this cloud processing is your consent, which you give by switching on data sharing and can withdraw at any time. Withdrawing consent doesn't affect processing that already happened before you withdrew it.

06

Community chat & user content

The community chat — the "Break Room" — is a shared, cloud-based space that becomes available when you turn on data sharing.

  • What's visible: messages you post in the Break Room, along with your display name and profile picture, are visible to other eligible users of the App. Please don't share anything there you wouldn't want others to see.
  • Moderation: you can report or block other users. We use reports and blocks to keep the community safe — for example, to review content and take action against abuse or violations.
  • Your responsibility: you're responsible for the content you post. Keep it lawful and respectful of others.
07

Third parties & service providers

We keep our list of third parties short. These providers process data on our behalf to make the App work — they're our "data processors" (GDPR) / "service providers" (CCPA), and they're not permitted to use your data for their own purposes.

Google Firebase (Google LLC)

We use Google Firebase for our core backend:

  • Authentication — email/password and anonymous sign-in.
  • Cloud Firestore (database) — your profile and reading progress (when you've opted into data sharing), and community chat messages.
  • Cloud Storage — delivering book content.
  • Cloud Messaging — delivering push notifications.

Google acts as a data processor / service provider for us. You can read Google's privacy policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

Apple

Apple is the platform and distributor channel for the App (the App Store) and handles push-notification delivery to Apple devices through the Apple Push Notification service (APNs).

What we don't do. The App uses no third-party advertising networks and no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs. We do not sell or rent your personal information.

Payments. The App currently has no in-app purchases and processes no payment data. (The physical book and e-book may be sold separately on the book website — that's outside this App and this policy.)

08

Push notifications

The App can send push notifications using Firebase Cloud Messaging. Notifications are optional.

  • If you enable notifications, a device push token is processed by Google and Apple so we can deliver messages to your device.
  • You can turn notifications off at any time in your device settings. If you turn them off, we stop sending them.
09

Device permissions

The App only asks for a permission at the moment it's needed, and only for these two things:

  • Photo Library — requested only when you choose to set a profile photo, so you can pick one from your library.
  • Notifications — requested only if you choose to enable push notifications.

The App does not request access to your camera, location, contacts, or microphone.

How we store, secure, and retain data; international transfers

Where your data lives

  • On your device: your reading progress, saved highlights, preferences, and your profile before you opt into data sharing all stay on your device.
  • In the cloud (only after you opt in): your synced profile, reading progress, and community chat messages are stored on Google Firebase.

Retention

We keep cloud data for as long as your account exists. It's deleted or purged when you delete your account, or when you withdraw data-sharing consent (turning the setting off purges the synced copy from the server). Device-local data stays on your device until you delete it or uninstall the App.

Security

We use reasonable measures to protect your data: it's encrypted in transit, and backend access is restricted by security rules so users can only read and write their own data (plus the shared community chat). No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, so we can't guarantee absolute security — but we work to protect your information.

International transfers

We process data on Google Firebase infrastructure, which may store and process data in the United States. If you use the App from outside the U.S., your information may be transferred to, stored in, and processed in the U.S. and other countries where our providers operate. Where required, such transfers rely on appropriate safeguards (for example, standard contractual clauses). By using the App and opting into cloud features, you understand your data may be processed in these locations.

Account deletion & your choices

Delete your account right inside the App. Go to Settings → Delete account. This deletes your authentication record and associated profile data.

You can also email us at jeremiahglover@path-and-pace.com to request deletion, and we'll take care of it.

Other choices you have:

  • Turn off data sharing — stops cloud sync and purges the synced copy from our servers.
  • Manage permissions — grant or revoke photo-library and notification access any time in your device settings.
  • Edit your profile — update your display name, phone number, or profile picture in the App.

Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you have rights over your personal information. We honor these rights for all users where we can, and you can exercise any of them by emailing jeremiahglover@path-and-pace.com. We won't discriminate against you for exercising your rights.

RightWhat it means
AccessAsk for a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
CorrectionAsk us to fix information that's inaccurate or incomplete.
DeletionAsk us to delete your information (also available in-app via Settings → Delete account).
PortabilityAsk for a copy of your data in a portable, machine-readable format.
Withdraw consentTurn off data sharing at any time to stop cloud processing and purge synced data.

For users in the EU & UK (GDPR / UK GDPR)

You have the rights above, plus the right to object to or restrict certain processing, and the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. As explained in Section 5, our legal basis for cloud processing is your consent, which you can withdraw at any time.

For California residents (CCPA / CPRA)

You have the right to know what personal information we collect and how we use it, to access and delete it, to correct it, and to be free from discrimination for exercising these rights. We do not sell your personal information, and we do not "share" it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We don't use your data for targeted advertising at all. To exercise your rights, email jeremiahglover@path-and-pace.com.

We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request, and we'll respond within the timeframe required by law.
