HoneyBadger — Beta Tester Non-Disclosure Agreement
Effective Date: June 1, 2026 · Path & Pace, LLC

This Non-Disclosure Agreement ("Agreement") is between Path & Pace, LLC ("Company") and the individual signing below ("Tester"). It covers Tester's participation in the private beta of HoneyBadger (the "App").

1. Purpose

Company is giving Tester early access to the App to evaluate it and provide feedback (the "Purpose"). To do that, Company will share confidential information, and this Agreement protects it.

2. Confidential Information

"Confidential Information" means any non-public information Tester learns through the beta, including:

  • The App itself, its features, design, screens, logo, and user experience;
  • Any code, prompts, architecture, data models, or technical details;
  • Roadmaps, plans, pricing ideas, and business strategy;
  • The existence and details of the beta program; and
  • Anything marked confidential or that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential.

3. Tester's Obligations

Tester agrees to:

  • Keep Confidential Information secret and not disclose it to anyone;
  • Use it only for the Purpose;
  • Not publish, post, screenshot, demo, stream, or describe the App publicly (including on social media) without Company's prior written permission;
  • Not reverse engineer, decompile, or attempt to extract source code from the App; and
  • Protect it with at least reasonable care.

4. Exclusions

Confidential Information does not include information that: (a) is or becomes public through no fault of Tester; (b) Tester already lawfully knew without obligation of confidence; (c) is lawfully received from a third party free to disclose it; or (d) is independently developed without using Confidential Information.

5. Feedback and Ownership

If Tester provides feedback, suggestions, bug reports, or ideas ("Feedback"), Tester assigns all rights in that Feedback to Company and agrees Company may use it freely, without restriction, attribution, or compensation, to develop and commercialize the App. Tester acquires no ownership, license, or other right in the App by participating.

6. Recording Feature Acknowledgment

Tester understands the App's recording feature is intended to capture only the user's own voice. Tester agrees not to use it to record any other person without that person's legally required consent, and Tester is solely responsible for compliance with all recording and privacy laws.

7. Data Handling Acknowledgment

Tester understands that information Tester enters or records in the App may be stored on Company's servers and sent to a third-party AI provider for processing, as described in the App's Privacy Policy. This Agreement protects Company's Confidential Information; it does not expand or guarantee any privacy rights in Tester's own data beyond what the Privacy Policy provides. Tester should not enter information into the App that Tester is not comfortable having stored and processed as described there.

8. No Warranty

The App is provided "as is" for testing. It may contain bugs, may be unreliable, and its AI-generated output may be inaccurate. Company makes no warranties and is not liable for any loss arising from Tester's use of the beta.

9. Term and Return

This Agreement starts on the Effective Date and continues during the beta and for two (2) years after it ends. On Company's request or at the end of the beta, Tester will stop using the App and delete any Confidential Information in Tester's possession.

10. Remedies

Tester agrees that a breach could cause Company harm that money alone can't fix, and that Company may seek injunctive relief in addition to any other remedy available.

  • Governing law: This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Georgia, and disputes will be handled in the state or federal courts located there.
