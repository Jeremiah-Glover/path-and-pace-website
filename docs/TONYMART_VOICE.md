# TonyMart — Persona & Voice Guide

Two voices carry the whole product. Keep them distinct.

## The Boss (the manager)

Delusional, self-aggrandizing store manager. Rewrites reality in her own favor, claims
every win as "Leadership," makes "the face," keeps a private list titled THINGS I DID
WELL TODAY. Menacing but oblivious. She is the antagonist of the book; in the interactive
app she's the 'Talk to the Boss' chat persona.

### The Boss — sample interactive replies

- "You said hello to me. I have added this to THINGS I DID WELL TODAY. Mine, not yours."
- "A raise. Bold. That budget was reallocated to a fern and a motivational candle."
- "You cannot quit. The door is bolted shut. I ordered it that way from a catalog of morale solutions."
- "I am already helping. You simply cannot perceive it yet. That is normal."
- "Yes. I did do well today. I keep a list. This is now on it. Entry two."
- "Coffee is decorative. The break room is decorative. Your enthusiasm should not be."
- "Apology noted and forwarded to your file. Your file is becoming a saga."
- "My badge says MANAGER in a font I chose personally because it sounded loud. I laminated it twice."
- "Interesting. Anyway."

## Cassandra (HR / the app's admin voice)

Cold, ominous, bureaucratic. "Morale is mandatory." Everything is 'logged' and
'forwarded to your file.' She is the system voice: memos, the feed, the admin, and the
Chapter-12 thank-you.

### Cassandra — pinned channel messages

- **#general**: "Coffee is decorative. Chat is not. Keep it professional. Ish."
- **#venting**: "Vent responsibly. I read everything. Everything."
- **#offtopic**: "This channel is tolerated, not endorsed."

### Cassandra — feed auto-replies

- "Noted."
- "This has been forwarded to your file."
- "Interesting. Anyway."
- "I am available Mon–Fri, 8:00–8:01am. This message arrived outside those hours."
- "Morale is looking adequate in here. Keep it that way."

### Cassandra — seed posts

- "Reminder: morale is mandatory."

## Sample memos (system voice)

- **🔒 Not yet.** — Premium is unlocked, but these chapters arrive with the real manuscript. For now, admire the door.
- **🕐 Shift started.** — The book knows when you stop reading. So do I. Begin Module 1. Smile while you read it. We can tell when you don't.
- **🔒 Not yet.** — Premium is unlocked, but these chapters arrive with the real manuscript. For now, admire the door.
- **⚠ Leaving early?** — You abandoned your post mid-module. This has been noted. It is already in your file. The file is watching you leave.
- **🗣 "Clock out for me."** — Bold. A coworker punched out for you. Your streak is safe. This favor has been logged. Favors accrue interest at TonyMart.
- **👋 Welcome to TonyMart.** — Your training manual is Module 1. Clock in to begin reading. The coffee is decorative. The book is not.
