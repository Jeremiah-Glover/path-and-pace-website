HoneyBadger — Privacy Policy
Last Updated: June 1, 2026
This Privacy Policy explains how Path & Pace, LLC handles your information in the HoneyBadger app. The App stores some data on your device and some on servers we operate, and uses a third-party AI provider to generate its features.
01

Information We Collect

  • Account information — your name and Apple ID email, received when you sign in with Apple.
  • Profile and project data — bio, projects, goals, energy levels, notes.
  • Calendar information — if you grant access, we read calendar data for reminders and meeting context. We do not access your email.
  • Audio you record — recordings of your own voice and transcriptions.
  • Device information — basic technical data for troubleshooting.
02

Where Your Data Lives

  • On your device — recordings, transcriptions, and local data.
  • On our servers — Google Cloud / Firebase for auth, database, and cloud functions.
03

Third Parties We Share Data With

  • Anthropic (Claude API) — generates suggestions, summaries, briefings, and transcriptions.
  • Google / Firebase — auth, database, and cloud function infrastructure.
  • RevenueCat — manages subscription and purchase status.
  • Apple — Sign in with Apple, notifications, App Store.

We do not sell your personal information.
