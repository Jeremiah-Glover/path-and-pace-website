This is the written consent form Path & Pace uses to enroll recipients in its text message program. It is completed and signed at onboarding. Path & Pace retains the signed copy. This page shows the blank form.

What the program sends

Path & Pace LLC operates an internal business operations platform that sends automated notifications by text message. Messages are generated by the platform, not written or sent individually by a person. They consist of:

  • Daily operations briefs
  • Task and deadline reminders
  • System and deployment status alerts
  • Account security notices

Path & Pace sends no marketing or promotional text messages under this program.

Program details

  • Message frequency varies, based on activity in your account.
  • Path & Pace charges nothing for these messages. Message and data rates may apply from your mobile carrier.
  • Reply STOP, STOPALL, UNSUBSCRIBE, CANCEL, END, or QUIT to stop messages. You will get one confirmation and nothing after that.
  • Reply HELP or INFO for assistance, or email support@path-and-pace.com.
  • You must be 18 or older and be the account holder or an authorized user of the mobile number you give.
  • Delivery depends on mobile carrier networks. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Consent is not a condition of employment, engagement, or of purchasing anything from Path & Pace. You may decline and still be onboarded, and may withdraw consent at any time.
