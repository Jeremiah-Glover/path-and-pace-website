SMS messaging program

Program name: Meridian Operational Notifications
Who receives it: the business owner of Path & Pace LLC only
Message frequency: daily

What the program is

Path & Pace operates an internal assistant that sends a short daily operational summary by text message — what its scheduled process produced and what is waiting for review — with a link to the internal workspace where the detail lives.

Who is enrolled

The sole recipient is the business owner, using his own mobile number, which he entered himself into the application's encrypted configuration. There is no signup form, no list, and no mechanism through which any customer, visitor, or third party could be enrolled. The destination number is a fixed configuration value, not a user-supplied field.
